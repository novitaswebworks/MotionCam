# Community Code of Conduct

We want MotionCam to be a welcoming place for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and points of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and remain open to correction.
- Respect privacy. Do not post someone else's personal information or recording without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report a concern privately to a maintainer. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

This code applies to project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
